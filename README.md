# Netflix-Data-Analysis-Project

- Business Analysis project of Netflix viewer and subscription data. Complete with advanced, multi-CTE SQL query, visualizations and data analysis. Topics and skills demonstrated include missing data imputation logic, metric correlations, and business recommendations. 
- Full project including data sets, interview questions and business write-up can be found in the Project folder.

Dataset & Business Analysis
Summary of Files in Datasets&Analysis Folder:
- Dataset: Dataset used for Analysis and Correlations related to the BI-Questions, data cleaning and imputation for missing datasets.
- BI - Questions: Business questions & answers related to insights driven from the Dataset.
- BI - Competitive Analysis & Viz: Competitive analysis and write-up of Netflix subscription churn, market share analysis, & consumer trends in the on-demand streaming service market and Tableau dashboards.
- BI - Instructions & Data Dictionary: Background of the exercise & Data Dictionary defining all of our data elements. 

Goal:
This exercise was completed by me without use of any AI tools for assistance and the goal is to demonstrate my Business & Data Analysis skills, techncial SQL ability using CASE statements, CTEs and WINDOW functions effectively, Excel proficiency, and most importantly communication and ability to derive business-related insights from datasets using plain-english and oraganized, easily-digestible visualizations created within Tableau.
